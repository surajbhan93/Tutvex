## 🎨 Design Philosophy

### Color Palette
- **Primary:** Indigo (`#6366f1`) and Purple (`#7c3aed`)
- **Success:** Emerald (`#10b981`)
- **Info:** Blue (`#3b82f6`)
- **Warning:** Amber (`#f59e0b`)
- **Neutral:** Gray scale for text and borders

### Visual Elements
- **Rounded Corners:** 14-20px for modern feel
- **Shadows:** Layered shadows for depth
- **Gradients:** Linear gradients for CTAs and headers
- **Icons:** Lucide icons with consistent sizing
- **Transitions:** Smooth 0.2-0.3s transitions

### Typography
- **Font Family:** Outfit (primary), Plus Jakarta Sans (accent)
- **Weights:** 400 (normal), 500 (medium), 600 (semibold), 700 (bold), 800 (extrabold)
- **Sizes:** 
  - Small text: 11-12px
  - Body: 14-15px
  - Labels: 14-16px
  - Headings: 16-32px
